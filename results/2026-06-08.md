### 1. 🔗 EU–UK ETS Linkage: July 13 Summit Seen as Catalyst for Major UKA Re-Rating
**Markets: UKA, EUA**


The EU-UK summit, reportedly scheduled for July 13, is expected to confirm a formal link between the two emissions trading systems — which would be the most significant structural change to European carbon markets since the UK left the EU ETS in 2021.
 
The EUA-UKA spread had already narrowed to below €13/t — its lowest since early February — as markets anticipate the announcement, with UKA prices surging close to 6% on May 26 alone, compared with a 1% rise in EUAs.



Energy Aspects forecasts UKA prices to average £74/t in H2 2026 supported by the linkage announcement, while EUAs are expected to remain rangebound around €75–80/t in the near term with policy reform uncertainty limiting directional moves until the Commission publishes EU ETS proposals in July.
 Downside risk: 
Uncertainty around Keir Starmer's tenure as prime minister has weighed on UKA prices; if the summit is pushed back to end-2026, Energy Aspects forecasts UKA prices to average only £57/t in H2 2026.


**📌 Portfolio implication:** The July 13 date is a hard near-term catalyst. Long UKA vs. short EUA spread trade most directly exposed. Summit delay or collapse is the key tail risk.

*(Energy Aspects, June 3, 2026)*

### 2. 🏗️ WCA/CCA: Washington Files Formal Linkage Regulations with California-Quebec (WCI)
**Markets: WCA, CCA**


On June 1, 2026, the Washington Department of Ecology proposed regulatory amendments to support the future linkage of Washington's Cap-and-Invest Program with the joint California-Quebec carbon market under the WCI, marking a formal step after the draft linkage agreement released in March 2026. The proposal would align key market rules including compliance timelines, allowance tracking, and accounting procedures across the three jurisdictions.



This could represent a significant shift for Washington's program, which has historically operated as a standalone market with tighter supply-demand conditions and higher allowance prices. Linkage may increase market liquidity, reduce price volatility, and narrow Washington's pricing premium relative to the broader WCI market.
 
California is expected to adopt amendments in September, while Quebec's proposal is under a 45-day consultation ending early July, with final regulations expected by end of summer.
 Public comments on the WA proposal are open until July 17, 2026.

**📌 Portfolio implication:** Bearish WCA on a standalone basis if/when linkage occurs; WCA prices should converge downward toward CCA. Timeline is 2027 operational start. Watch California's September adoption.

*(Carbon Pulse / WA Dept. of Ecology — June 2, 2026)*

### 6. ⚡ RGGI (Second-Order): New York Legislature Passes Data Center Moratorium Bill
**Markets: RGGI (second-order)**

The bill creates a one-year pause on permits for large data centers defined as facilities with a peak demand of 20 MW or more.
 
Governor Hochul has expressed reservations about a statewide moratorium and has not yet said whether she will sign or veto the bill.


**📌 Portfolio implication:** Data centers are a rapidly growing source of electricity demand in NY, which is RGGI-covered.
