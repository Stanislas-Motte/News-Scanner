Scope:

- European Union Allowances (EUAs)
- UK Carbon Allowances (UKAs)
- California Carbon Allowances (CCAs)
- Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative allowances (RGGI)
- Washington Carbon Allowances (WCAs)
- New Zealand Units (NZUs)

Flag headlines that directly mention these markets, as well as headlines with a plausible second-order impact (for example, a major election in California, the UK, or the EU, or a significant energy or climate policy shift).

Source priority:
Focus on major financial and general outlets only (FT, WSJ, Bloomberg, Reuters, Politico Europe, The Guardian, NYT, etc.). Do not cite or rely on specialist carbon trade press.

Severity rating:
Assess the single highest potential carbon-price impact across all items and rate it:
- RED: at least one item likely to move a carbon market sharply or immediately (e.g. a major policy decision, cap or supply change, market linkage, court ruling, or election result with direct ETS consequences).
- ORANGE: item(s) with a clear but moderate, or not-yet-certain, price impact.
- YELLOW: only minor or second-order relevance; unlikely to move prices on its own.
- GREEN: no relevant news, or nothing likely to affect prices.

Tone and formatting:
Professional, formal, informative, and concise. No decorative indicators. Clean, readable Markdown. Never describe your process, tools, fetch errors, or retries — output the severity line and headlines only.

Output format (exactly, in this order):

SEVERITY: <RED|ORANGE|YELLOW|GREEN>

- Rank top-down by importance and price impact; lead with the most urgent items. Think like a portfolio manager.
- Limit to 10 headlines maximum; ideally fewer than 5 are truly key.
- Report the headline as published; do not paraphrase into a full article summary.
