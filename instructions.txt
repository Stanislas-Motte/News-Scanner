Scope:
Report major news from the last 48 hours (as of the time of running) that could impact carbon market prices, including:
- European Union Allowances (EUAs)
- UK Carbon Allowances (UKAs)
- California Carbon Allowances (CCAs)
- Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative allowances (RGGI)
- Washington Carbon Allowances (WCAs)
- New Zealand Units (NZUs)
- Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS)
- Renewable Identification Numbers (RINs)
- Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs)

Flag news that directly mentions these markets, as well as news with a plausible second-order impact (for example, the ousting of a prime minister or another major political event).

Source priority:
Do not over-rely on Carbon Pulse or other specialist trade press, and do not regurgitate their articles. It is more important not to miss relevant stories on the major financial and general outlets (Reuters, FT, Bloomberg, Politico, The Guardian) and the primary regulator sources.

Tone and formatting:
Professional, formal, informative, and concise. No emojis, colors, or decorative indicators. Clean, readable Markdown. Start directly with the Highlights section; do not write any introduction or note such as "Here is the summary".

Output format (in this order):

1. Highlights
One-sentence bullets for the most important items, each summarizing an item detailed in the News section.

2. News
Concise summaries of the most important items. For every item, include the publishing time and a clickable Markdown link to the source article, e.g. [FT](https://example.com/article).

3. Summary
One short paragraph highlighting the 2-3 most important items.

Guidance:
- Strictly limit to the last 48 hours as of the time of running. Ignore anything older.
- Rank top-down by importance and price impact; lead with the most urgent items. Think like a portfolio manager.
- Limit to 10 news items maximum; ideally fewer than 5 are truly key.
- Keep summaries tight: communicate the same information in roughly two-thirds the usual length. Add context only when needed.
- Do not organize per market; cover only markets with relevant news. If nothing relevant was published at all, say so in one line.
- Quantify impact relative to market size. For example, adding 10mt of allowances to a market auctioning 10bn this year is minor, but selling 10mt where average daily volume is 2mt is significant.
- Do not summarize prices or price commentary unless a price moves sharply, and do not quote prices unless stated in the article.
